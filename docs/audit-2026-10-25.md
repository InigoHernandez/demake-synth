# Demake audit, 2026-10-25

The owner asked for a usability, workflow and bug audit.

Five read-only reviews ran in parallel:
- usability and workflow
- sequencer, arp and chords
- sound engine
- saving and state
- device glue, MIDI and the website

They built probes under ASan and UBSan, and the sequencer review also ran a fuzzer. Each finding below was checked again before it was fixed. Every fix has a regression test in `host/dmk_host.c --selftest`.

## Fixed

Commits: 6afce39, 144d720, and the one after them.

### Notes left on
- **The arp ate a note-off.** A note-off now ends its own voice before asking the arp. Before, the arp took the off of a key held while the arp came on, when the roll fed it that pitch.
- **Arps after a new or opened project.** A new or opened project now lets every arp go. Before, a held arp sounded on, and its notes carried into the next project.
- **A held arp after STOP.** STOP now lets a held arp drop what the roll and the chords fed it. A key still held plays on.
- **MIDI harmony.** It is counted per held note. Two channels on one pitch used to leave the follower's note on.
- **The chords' Vary.** It never doubles a tone now.

### Takes
- **A key held across a stop and a start.** It is no longer written; it used to come out 255 steps long.
- **A key held across a pattern switch.** It is no longer written into the next pattern.
- **Latency.** A take subtracts the platform's latency: 1280 samples on the FM-1, and the browser's own measured latency in the emulator. Before, keys landed late by the output latency.
- **A tempo raised inside a step.** It now runs one step, not several at once.
- **The roll's cursor.** It stays inside a shorter pattern that is switched in. Before, a note written past the end never played.
- **A song take.** It counts past 255 bars.

### Saving
- **A save past its sector.** `song_pack` checks the room left before every byte. Before, a full song wrote past its 4 KiB buffer.
- **Long user chords.** User chords up to 16 half-bars come back as written; 9 to 16 used to load as 2.
- **Notes past a shortened end.** They survive a save.
- **Too many notes.** The screen says "Too many notes to save", not "Not saved".
- **A newer firmware's project.** It is listed as used and named "Newer firmware". It is never opened or saved over. Before, it showed as empty and the next save replaced it.
- **The boot.** If the newest project fails to load, it tries the next newest. If none loads, the work open goes to an empty slot.
- **OCT− twice.** It deletes only the project it was armed on, and it does nothing while playing. On Sounds it inits only the track it was armed on.
- **A rename.** It waits for STOP before writing.
- **Ready.** REC's ready state goes with the work left behind.
- **The roll's stored bytes.** They are checked on load; a zoom byte past 2 used to divide by zero.
- **Older songs.** Songs of versions 1 to 3 reset the harmony intervals.
- **Projects while playing.** PLAY on Projects stops the transport, as anywhere. Before, two presses replaced the work with a new project.
- **Changes not saved.** The first PLAY on another slot says "Unsaved: PLAY again to lose it".

### MIDI
- **Notes coming in.** They are never sent back out. A loop through the port used to repeat a note forever.
- **The clock out.** Its first clock comes with the start.
- **The clock in.** Demake starts on the first clock after a start, which is MIDI's downbeat. If no clock follows, it starts at once at its own tempo.

### Panel and screen
- **Held buttons.**
  - They say what they do: EDIT, SEQ and ARP.
  - The others show nothing, and a slow press acts as a tap.
- **Cut words.** The text audit fails anything shortened with an ellipsis. The tour shows every choice of every field, every progression and every recording's name. Everything it found was shortened to fit.
- **Wording.** The Chord page's foot says "Recording" while recording. Confirmations say what they will clear. Writing a chord into a preset says which User slot took its copy.
- **LEDs.** Save and the Song page light their buttons.
- **The flash erase.** It plays silence. Before, the DMA looped the last 11 ms of sound during the 50–400 ms erase.
- **The roll's K3.** It is now Follow, the owner's request.
- **FX > Space.** Its ALGO and PRESET set the shared reverb. The reverb redesign below replaces this.

### Emulator and pages
- **The keys after the volume.** The keys still play after the volume is touched.
- **The power button.** It tries again after a failed start.
- **The manual.** The version, the scenes and SELECT are corrected.

## In progress: the owner's redesign
The owner chose:
- **A reverb on each track:** Room, Plate, Spring and a small Hall.
- **Colour on the mix**, no longer on each track.

That work is being done in a separate copy of the code (a worktree). The sound engine's remaining findings go with it:
- **The echo's time:** it wraps above knob 105.
- **The grains:** they break at SIZE 124 and above.
- **DC offsets:** some tails never settle to zero, in the Room, Dictaphone, Radio, Phaser, Cassette and Crush.
- **The mixer:** it overflows at extreme settings.
- **Left shifts of negative values:** in Crush and Flanger.
- **The echo's TIME knob:** it zippers when turned.
- **CPU:** the cost grows with transposition, up to 10,700 instructions a sample at the top notes.
- **Fine tune:** it clamps for some sounds.
- **Levels:** some effects sit far from the dry level.

The old shared reverbs' own bugs go with them: Freeze running away, Shimmer's DC latch and Hall's int32 overflow.

## Known, not fixed yet
- **Saving over a project is not atomic.** The sector is erased, then written. A power cut inside those ~50 ms loses the project; the library's sector holds all 32 sounds. The fix is a spare sector, with the newest valid copy winning.
- **Generations wrap after 65,535 saves.** The boot would then open a stale project.
- **A record is applied while it is read.** One that passes its CRC but ends early leaves a mix. No firmware path writes such a record.
- **On an outside clock, takes lose their micro timing.** The fraction of the step isn't known there.
- **On the FM-1, keys are read once per loop pass.** A tap shorter than a frame's drawing can be lost. MIDI in and out wait for the frame. This is unconfirmed on the device; reading the note edges would fix it.
- **SELECT's 120 ms filter.** It came from the old knob decoder and may now drop real fast clicks.
- **Two emulator tabs** overwrite each other's saves.
- **The installer.** A failed resume reports "No FM-1 answered".
- **Room in a project.** It holds about 630 notes; a denser format or two sectors would hold more.

## For the owner to decide
1. **Step entry, then PLAY.** With REC lit and stopped, the keys write steps, and PLAY then waits for a key (Ready) instead of playing. To just listen: REC off, then PLAY; or PLAY twice. Should PLAY play at once when keys wrote steps since REC was lit?
2. **A roll that isn't heard.** A synth that plays the chords still draws its roll. Writing a note on it takes it off the chords, with only a notice. Should it draw that roll in grey and ask first?
3. **A new scene shares its patterns.** An empty scene copies the one playing, and its patterns are the same ones, so editing scene 2's roll changes scene 1's. A new pattern for the scene is two hidden levels deep: the Song page's Scene row, then OCT+. Should SEQ held + OCT+ give the scene a new pattern?
4. **SELECT.** On Home, Roll, Filter and Amp it picks the synth. On Sound, FX, LFO, Chord and Global it scrolls the rows, and the synth needs HOME + SELECT. Should it pick the synth everywhere, with the page button cycling the rows?
5. **Dead knobs.**
   - ALGO on Sound > Voice and Global > Setup.
   - K3 and K4 on LFO > Sync and Chord > Keys.
   - The knobs of an effect that is off still change its hidden values.
6. **Undo and the library.**
   - There is no undo.
   - Kept sounds can't be deleted, overwritten or named. After 32 the library is full for good.
