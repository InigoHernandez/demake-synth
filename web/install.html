01Connect

Plug it in, switch it on, press Connect.

Use a USB cable that carries data. Allow MIDI when the browser asks: that is how the FM‑1 answers. Close DAWs and other installer tabs first.

Ask the FM‑1 what it runs

02Install

Press Install, then wait until it says done.

  1. 1The running firmware checks the package and stages the update loader.
  2. 2The FM‑1 restarts into its update mode.
  3. 3The loader writes Demake, about a minute, and the FM‑1 starts again into it.

03If it stops
1Not found

Check the cable carries data and MIDI is allowed. Reload, then connect again.

2Stopped writing

It stays in update mode until done. Replug and press Install: the page finishes it.

3No screen

Switch it off and on. Still dark? Hold OCT− as you switch on: a rescue mode this page works from.

04Going back

Back to M‑VAVE's own firmware, at any time.

Choose the official V15 FM-1.fwsc from M‑VAVE, unmodified: it is checked here and sent nowhere. Other firmware is at your own risk; the rescue above and M‑VAVE's updater are the ways back.

05What it writes

Its own slot, and only its own parts of the data area.

  1. The appDemake and its sounds. It replaces what ran before.
  2. 0x93000–0xAFFFFKept for the factory sounds the app has no room for.
  3. 0xB0000–0xBFFFFSongs and settings, once saving is built; may write over other data.
  4. 0xC0000–0xDFFFFLeft alone: MONO2K's saves stay.
