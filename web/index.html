03Sound

Your own recordings under every synth, worn by tape, codec and a room.

A layer from your samples under each sound. Crush, codec smear, tape, cassette, VHS and chorus, then reverbs voiced after console rooms and halls. Integer DSP, on both of the FM‑1's cores.

05Workflow
1Keys write

The keys play the current synth. On Roll and Chord, stopped, a key writes at the cursor; playing, REC records it in time.

2One job a knob

SELECT the synth, PRESETS its sound, ALGORITHM the left cell, K1–K4 the four on top. The same on every page.

3Tap, again, hold

Tap a page button to open it, tap again for its next row, hold it for its layer: its map shows on screen.

4Hold, then act

A held key is a note, SEQ held the sequence, HOME held the patterns. OCT− takes away, OCT+ adds a copy.

5Everything undoes

GLO with OCT− undoes, sixteen steps back; GLO with OCT+ redoes. Only writing over another save asks first.

6Nothing hidden

What a held button does is on screen while you hold it; the keys' lights show the chord now.
