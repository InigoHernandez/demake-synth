An ambient lofi machine for the M-VAVE FM-1

Three synths, a chord machine, and the snow outside.

Write a melody with the keys, let the chords carry it, and wear it down through tape, cassette and a console's reverb. This is phase 0: the screen's shell and a sine a key.

The instrument

Three synths

Sample, pad, drone, pluck

Each with its own sequence in a piano roll, and a layer from the owner's own recordings under it.

Eight patterns

Switch them live

Hold HOME, press a key: the next pattern comes in at the bar. Sounds stay, the notes and chords change.

The wear

Colour, media, motion, space

Crush, codec smear, tape, cassette, VHS, chorus, and reverbs voiced after console rooms and halls.
